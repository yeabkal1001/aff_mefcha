# Layer 1 — CEFR Curriculum Domains



## A2 — Elementary Speaker

### A2-D01 — Personal Life

**Description**

Communicate about oneself and familiar people in everyday situations.

#### Learning Objectives

* Introduce yourself confidently.  
* Describe family and friends.  
* Talk about daily routines.  
* Express basic likes and dislikes.  
* Describe personal experiences.

#### Practice Contexts

* Introducing yourself  
* Meeting someone new  
* Family gathering  
* Talking about hobbies  
* Daily routine  
* Weekend activities

#### Required Competencies

Each requirement carries a role. **Core** competencies are what the domain exists to teach, and their mastery gates domain completion, so their sub-competencies are listed explicitly. **Supporting** competencies are needed to perform the domain but are not its purpose. **Incidental** competencies are measured whenever they appear but are never the reason a session is scheduled. Supporting and incidental requirements expand automatically to all CEFR-appropriate sub-competencies of the parent.

##### Vocabulary

* V001 Personal Information — **core** — V001.01, V001.02, V001.03, V001.05  
* V003 Daily Life & Routine — **core** — V003.01, V003.03, V003.07  
* V002 Family & Relationships — supporting  
* V014 Hobbies & Leisure — supporting  
* V015 Emotions & Feelings — supporting  
* V016 Personality & Character — incidental

##### Grammar

* G001 Be Verb — **core** — G001.01, G001.03, G001.05  
* G003 Pronouns — **core** — G003.01, G003.04  
* G005 Present Simple — **core** — G005.01, G005.04, G005.05  
* G006 Present Continuous — **core** — G006.01, G006.05  
* G002 Articles — supporting  
* G009 Adjectives & Adverbs — supporting  
* G004 Nouns & Quantity — incidental

##### Pronunciation

* P005 Word Stress — **core** — P005.01, P005.03  
* P001 Individual Consonant Sounds — supporting — P001.08 only; P001.01–P001.07 are `observable: false`  
* P003 Sound Discrimination — supporting  
* P014 Speaking Pace — supporting  
* P002 Individual Vowel Sounds — incidental  
* P004 Syllables — incidental

##### Fluency

* F002 Describing — **core** — F002.01, F002.06  
* F003 Answering Questions — **core** — F003.01  
* F001 Naming & Identification — supporting  
* F004 Asking Questions — supporting

G006 Present Continuous was added to this domain. Introducing yourself requires *I am studying* as much as *I study*, and the contrast between the two is the most common error at this level for Amharic speakers.
