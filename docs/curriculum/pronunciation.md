# Master Pronunciation Library


---

## P001 — Individual Consonant Sounds

**CEFR:** A2–C2

**Description**

Accurately produce the consonant sounds of English.

### Sub-Competencies

* P001.01 Plosives (/p/, /b/, /t/, /d/, /k/, /g/)  
* P001.02 Fricatives (/f/, /v/, /s/, /z/, /ʃ/, /ʒ/)  
* P001.03 Affricates (/tʃ/, /dʒ/)  
* P001.04 Nasals (/m/, /n/, /ŋ/)  
* P001.05 Liquids (/l/, /r/)  
* P001.06 Glides (/w/, /j/)  
* P001.07 Dental Sounds (/θ/, /ð/)  
* P001.08 Common L1 Sound Confusions

## Recommended prerequisite graph

Unlike vocabulary, pronunciation has a very strong dependency chain. I recommend explicitly modeling it as a progression graph:

P012 Intonation  
        │  
        ▼  
P013 Pausing & Chunking  
        │  
        ▼  
P014 Speaking Pace  
        │  
        ▼  
P015 Expressiveness  
        │  
        ▼  
P018 Overall Pronunciation Control

`P016 (Listening-to-Pronunciation Awareness)` should support the entire graph rather than belong to a single point, because listening skills reinforce every pronunciation competency. Likewise, `P017 (Accent Adaptability)` should be treated as an advanced specialization that builds on overall pronunciation control rather than a prerequisite for it.
