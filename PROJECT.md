# AI Communication Coach

An AI speaking coach for Ethiopian Amharic-native English learners.

**What it does:** Voice conversations in English, with real-time corrections and explanations spoken back in Amharic. Every session measures what you moved — fluency, vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, confidence.

**Why it matters:** English is the language of Ethiopian universities, hospitality, tech, and remote work. But structured speaking practice costs 500–1,500 ETB/hour with a tutor. The coach delivers unlimited practice for 1,000 ETB/month — roughly 1/100th the cost.

**How it works:** Gemini drives the coaching dialogue, ElevenLabs + Addis AI provide bilingual voice (English + Amharic), Whisper analyses every word you speak. The result is a Communication Profile with real numbers that improve over time.

**Who it's for:** Ethiopian students preparing for university and professionals in hospitality, tech, and government who need real spoken English — not textbook English.

**Status:** MVP working, seeking investment to scale. Built with Next.js, Express, Prisma, Postgres, and five AI APIs.
